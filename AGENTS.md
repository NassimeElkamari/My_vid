# Wach 3aref reel style

The user approved the visual and editing style of Reel 4 as the reference for future Wach 3aref reels. Use `out/reel4.mp4`, `src/reel4/Reel4.tsx`, `src/reel4/ComicScenes.tsx`, and `src/reel4/MoreScenes.tsx` as practical references. Adapt the treatment to each topic and follow any newer creative direction from the user.

- Build lively educational stories with varied, distinct animated scenes and clear visual explanations. Avoid repeating supplied video clips and avoid long static holds.
- Use friendly 2D comic illustrations, bold dark outlines, expressive motion, and the Reel 4 palette of warm yellow `#FFD60A`, sky blue `#5AC8FA`, royal blue `#1E40FF`, soft cream, and dark `#111827`.
- Keep the narration central. Use concise, readable right-to-left Darija captions with active-word highlighting, Cairo Bold, and space for the Instagram interface. Verify captions and timing against the supplied audio and script when possible.
- Use smooth, purposeful transitions, animated infographic elements, and a thin yellow progress bar. Preserve the user's existing reels and assets when creating a new one.
